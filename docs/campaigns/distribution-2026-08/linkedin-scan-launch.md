## Post body

Your parents are never going to install a scam-checking app.

But they've been forwarding you emails since 2009.

So that's the interface we built on →

The people scammers target hardest are the people least likely to try new software. Every protection tool asks them to install something, sign up for something, learn something new.

Forwarding an email is the one move every Australian inbox already knows.

So now Arthur works like this:

— Forward any suspicious email to scan@askarthur.au
— About a minute later a reply lands in the same thread: the verdict, the red flags, and what to do next
— Free. No signup, no app, no new anything. Three checks a day.

The part I'm most pleased with: you can set it up for someone else. Sit down with Mum for five minutes, add scan@askarthur.au to her contacts, show her it's just the forward button she already uses. That's the whole onboarding.

Phishing cost Australians $97.6 million in 2025 (ACCC Targeting Scams Report, March 2026) — and email is still where so much of it starts.

Try it right now: find the dodgiest thing in your junk folder and forward it. See what comes back.

If it helps you once, tell one person. That's how this works.
